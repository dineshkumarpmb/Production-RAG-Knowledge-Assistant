# Travel and Expenses Policy

## Booking travel

Book flights and trains through the Northwind travel portal at least 14 days before departure. Economy class is standard for flights shorter than six hours.

## Hotels

Hotel stays are reimbursed up to 150 EUR per night, or up to 220 EUR per night in London, Paris and New York.

## Meals and per diem

The daily meal allowance is 45 EUR. Alcohol is not reimbursed.

## Submitting claims

Submit expense claims in the finance system within 30 days of the expense, with itemised receipts attached.
