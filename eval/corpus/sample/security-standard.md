# Information Security Standard

## Incident codes

SEC-4471: lost or stolen laptop. SEC-2209: suspected phishing email. SEC-3150: unauthorised access to a customer record. Report every incident to the service desk within one hour.

## Passwords

Passwords must contain at least 14 characters. Use the company password manager and never reuse a password across systems. Multi-factor authentication is mandatory for email and the VPN.

## Device encryption

All laptops must use full-disk encryption (BitLocker on Windows, FileVault on macOS). Unencrypted devices are blocked from the corporate network.
