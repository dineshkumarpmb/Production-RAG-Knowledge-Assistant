# Leave Policy

## Annual leave

Full-time employees receive 25 days of paid annual leave per calendar year. Part-time staff receive a pro-rata allowance. Up to five unused days may be carried into the next year and must be used by 31 March.

## Sick leave

If you are ill, tell your manager before 09:00 on your first day of absence. A doctor's note is required for absences longer than seven calendar days.

## Parental leave

Birth parents receive 26 weeks of fully paid leave. Partners receive 6 weeks of fully paid leave, which can be taken at any time within the first year.
