# Remote Work Guidelines

## Eligibility

Employees who have completed their probation period may work remotely up to three days per week with their manager's approval.

## Equipment

Northwind provides a laptop, a monitor and a headset. A one-off home-office allowance of 300 EUR covers a desk or chair.

## Working abroad

Working from another country is allowed for up to 20 working days per year and needs approval from HR, because of tax and insurance rules.
